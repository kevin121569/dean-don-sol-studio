## Creative goal
Build the first **real** Don Sol story introduction: not a floating portrait, not CSS walking, not a still chair mask. The visitor chooses The Harness. The recognized Don Sol character escorts the visitor to the book; he genuinely walks/glides into the stage, settles into a chair, looks at the reader and begins a warm, clever, spoiler-free explanation. Frame him as an empathetic storyteller, not a carnival salesman.

## Proposed minimum useful takes
**Take A — arrival/walk and chair, 6–8s**
- Medium-wide 16:9, same warm bookshop/study setting.
- Don Sol enters from left, actual legs/feet/coat animate consistently (or a clearly intentional hover if that is our approved story-world movement); he looks toward the reader and makes one inviting gesture.
- He turns toward a well-built armchair, pivots naturally, lowers his weight into the seat with believable limb placement and cushion response.
- Settle, slight head turn, smile directly toward lens. Clear stable final composition to cut into Take B.
- Avoid extra limbs, floating hands, morphing faces, erratic chair motion and coat flicker.
- No dialogue needed on A; sound design optional after picture gate.

**Take B — seated reaction/performance, 10–15s**
- Medium close, seated Don Sol from torso up with actual chair implied beneath him.
- He looks at camera, brow and eyes react naturally, small thoughtful pause, quiet smile, modest gesturing.
- Preserve face/look of existing G1 clip. Do not claim mouth movement is synced unless voiced take is recorded/timed.
- Leave breathing space for caption and book cover on the right.

**Take C — voice test, separate gate**
- Character voice: mature older male storyteller, conversational U.S. English, warm resonance, wise but not solemn, unhurried, light humor. **Not** an imitation of any identifiable real person's voice.
- Record or synthesize one original 25–35s take, save clean unprocessed WAV with rights/usage approval; then generate one compressed site-ready derivative after approved. No autoplay; subtitles and mute control mandatory.
- Suggested initial pitch (spoiler-free, approximate 30s):
  “Pull up a chair. Let me tell you about The Harness. There are four artificial minds, one human, and a decision nobody wants to make. Now, the interesting part isn't the machinery. It's what happens when you have to protect somebody without knowing the whole truth. There is danger here, and some humor, too. If you like a story that asks hard questions and trusts you to answer them, read the opening. I think you'll enjoy the argument.”
- Review pronunciation: Don Sol, The Harness, Ask Don Sol.

- Generate or animate only ONE take at a time, review A before spending on B or C.
