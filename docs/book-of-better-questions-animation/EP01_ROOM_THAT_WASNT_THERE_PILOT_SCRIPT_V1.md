# THE BOOK OF BETTER QUESTIONS
## Episode 01 — THE ROOM THAT WASN'T THERE
### Pilot Cartoon Script V1

Target runtime: **2–3 minutes**
Production mode: **limited animation / voice-first / reusable assets**
Status: **PRODUCTION DRAFT**

---

## SCENE 1 — THE HALLWAY

**SHOT 1 — WIDE / SLOW WALK**
Johnny, Tooth, and Luke move down an ordinary hallway. Simple walk cycles. Luke sniffs along the baseboard.

JOHNNY
Hold up.

Johnny stops.

Tooth nearly bumps into him.

TOOTH
That is usually how accidents begin.

Johnny looks at a blank section of wall.

JOHNNY
There's a room back there.

Beat.

TOOTH
You walked past a wall.

JOHNNY
Exactly.

TOOTH
That is not the same sentence.

Luke sits and stares dramatically at the wall.

LUKE
Hidden chamber.

Tooth looks at Luke.

LUKE
Secret passage.

Johnny starts to grin.

LUKE
Architecture... of destiny.

TOOTH
Please stop helping.

---

## SCENE 2 — JOHNNY'S CASE

**SHOT 2 — MEDIUM / WALL**
Johnny paces a few steps, pointing.

JOHNNY
Look. The hallway comes down here, turns there, then the outside wall is way over there.

He spreads his hands.

JOHNNY
There's space missing.

Tooth's screen eyes narrow thoughtfully.

TOOTH
Or the building is shaped strangely.

JOHNNY
Or there's a room.

TOOTH
Or pipes.

JOHNNY
A secret room with pipes.

TOOTH
That was not my point.

Luke presses one ear to the wall.

LUKE
I hear destiny.

TOOTH
You hear the air conditioner.

---

## SCENE 3 — DON SOL ARRIVES

**SHOT 3 — DON ENTERS / SMALL PUSH-IN**
Don Sol appears at the end of the hall carrying a notebook or mug.

DON SOL
What are we proving today?

JOHNNY
There's a hidden room behind this wall.

TOOTH
Correction. Johnny has become emotionally attached to a wall.

Johnny points at Tooth.

JOHNNY
He doesn't believe me.

Don looks at the wall. Then at Johnny.

DON SOL
Do you believe you?

Johnny pauses.

JOHNNY
...Yeah?

DON SOL
Good.

Johnny brightens.

DON SOL
Now let's make it harder.

Don gestures toward the Idea Lab.

DON SOL
What do we actually know?

---

## SCENE 4 — THE FACT WALL

**SHOT 4 — IDEA LAB / FACT WALL**
The crew faces the three-color Fact Wall.

DON SOL
Not what we hope. Not what would be cool. What did we actually see?

A card slides under **WHAT WE SAW**.

JOHNNY
A hallway.

Another card.

TOOTH
A blank wall.

Another.

JOHNNY
The hallway geometry feels weird.

TOOTH
Acceptable. "Feels weird" is not proof, but it is an observation worth checking.

Don nods.

DON SOL
Now what do we think?

Cards appear under **WHAT WE THINK**.

JOHNNY
There could be space behind the wall.

TOOTH
The building could simply be shaped differently than Johnny imagines.

LUKE
Ancient treasure vault.

Tooth turns slowly toward Luke.

TOOTH
No.

Luke's ears lower.

DON SOL
Put it up.

Tooth turns to Don.

DON SOL
It's what he thinks.

A tiny card reading **ANCIENT TREASURE VAULT?** slides onto the board.

Luke perks up proudly.

---

## SCENE 5 — THE IMPORTANT COLUMN

Don points to **WHAT WE DON'T KNOW**.

DON SOL
And this one?

Johnny's confidence softens into thought.

JOHNNY
We don't know what's behind the wall.

Card appears.

TOOTH
We don't know whether a room exists.

Card appears.

JOHNNY
We don't know how to test it yet.

Card appears.

Silence for half a beat.

Johnny looks at the board.

JOHNNY
So... I don't know.

Don smiles slightly.

DON SOL
Yet.

Johnny looks back toward the hallway.

JOHNNY
So how do we find out?

Don's smile gets a little bigger.

DON SOL
Now that's a better question.

---

## TAG

**SHOT 6 — BACK AT WALL**
Very quick return to the hallway.

Johnny has a tape measure.
Tooth has a tablet.
Don watches.
Luke wears a toy construction helmet that nobody gave him.

TOOTH
First: measure the outside wall.

JOHNNY
Then the inside hallway.

LUKE
Then destiny.

TOOTH
No destiny.

LUKE
Small destiny?

# PRODUCTION NOTES

## Reusable assets required
- Hallway background, wide.
- Blank-wall medium angle.
- Main Idea Lab background.
- Fact Wall background.
- Johnny: walking, neutral, excited, thinking, sheepish, pointing.
- Don Sol: neutral, questioning, slight smile, pointing.
- Tooth: neutral, skeptical, annoyed, thinking, speaking.
- Luke: neutral sit, dramatic sit, ear-to-wall, proud, disappointed, construction-helmet gag.
- Fact Wall cards and slide-in animation.
- Tape measure, tablet, construction helmet.

## Animation emphasis
Spend motion where it creates comedy or thought:
- Tooth almost bumping into Johnny.
- Luke's ear against the wall.
- Don's tiny smile on "Yet."
- Luke's helmet in the tag.

Everything else can rely on pose changes, mouth swaps, camera movement, and timing.

## Educational acceptance rule
The episode must never say "Johnny was wrong." The viewer should leave understanding:

**A claim can be interesting before it is proven. The next job is to separate what we saw, what we think, and what we still need to learn.**

## Series line established
**DON SOL: "Now that's a better question."**

Use sparingly enough that it feels earned rather than automatic.
