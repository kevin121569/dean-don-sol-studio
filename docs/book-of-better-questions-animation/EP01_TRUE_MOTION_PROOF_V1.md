# THE BOOK OF BETTER QUESTIONS — EPISODE 01
## TRUE MOTION PROOF V1

Status: **MOTION / VOICE BRIDGE TEST**

This file records the first short sequence produced after the full animatic specifically to test visible character motion plus audible dialogue.

## Scope
Approximate runtime: **11 seconds**.

Sequence:
1. Johnny: "There's a room back there."
2. Tooth: "You walked past a wall."
3. Johnny: "Exactly."
4. Tooth: "That is not the same sentence."
5. Luke: "Architecture... of destiny."

## What this proof is testing
- audible character dialogue,
- visible mouth / face motion,
- blink / reaction motion,
- small body / camera movement,
- distinct character timing,
- whether the approved illustrated look survives movement.

## What this proof is NOT
- final rigging,
- final lip sync,
- final voice casting,
- final walk cycles,
- final compositing,
- final sound design,
- final episode animation.

## Production conclusion
If the characters remain readable and appealing while speaking and moving, proceed to reusable character rigs / motion assets for the full pilot.

The full episode should then add:
- Johnny walking, pointing, reacting, and handling props,
- Tooth arm gestures, screen-face changes, and locomotion,
- Luke ear, tail, head, body, sniffing, and helmet actions,
- Don Sol hand gestures, head turns, facial reactions, and speaking poses,
- Fact Wall card movement,
- mouth sets / lip sync,
- ambience, music, and effects.

## Governing rule
**Story first. Movement where it earns its cost. Reuse everything possible.**
