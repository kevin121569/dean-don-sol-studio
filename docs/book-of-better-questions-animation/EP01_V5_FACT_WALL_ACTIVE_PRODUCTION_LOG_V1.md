## Purpose
Upgrade the Fact Wall from a static exposition screen into an active visual teaching mechanic while preserving the locked educational meaning.

## Production changes
- The three Fact Wall categories visibly activate in sequence:
  - WHAT WE SAW
  - WHAT WE THINK
  - WHAT WE DON'T KNOW
- A `WOULD BE COOL?` idea appears briefly and is rejected as evidence.
- The observed evidence cards then arrive and sort into the correct category.
- All three observed facts are now visibly grouped beneath **WHAT WE SAW**:
  - HALLWAY
  - BLANK WALL
  - GEOMETRY FEELS WEIRD
- The cue `WHAT DID WE ACTUALLY SEE?` reinforces the sorting rule.

## Teaching correction caught during QA
An earlier V5 attempt visually spread the three blue evidence cards across the lower width of the wall. Even though the cards were blue, their spatial placement could imply that BLANK WALL or GEOMETRY FEELS WEIRD belonged under WHAT WE THINK / WHAT WE DON'T KNOW.

That version was rejected.

V5.1 stacks all three evidence cards unmistakably under **WHAT WE SAW**. This follows the Memory Hook Learning Doctrine: the story and visual metaphor must preserve the real technical meaning.
