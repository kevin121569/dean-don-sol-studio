# Book of Better Questions — Body Motion Rig Proof V1

Status: PASS — first reusable body-motion module established.

## Purpose
Test reusable limited-animation body mechanics on the locked Johnny and Tooth designs before dropping those motions into Episode 1 scenes.

## Proof
The proof separates the body mechanics into three visible phases:

1. WALK CYCLE — body bob plus alternating limb motion.
2. STOP / SETTLE — characters absorb the stop instead of freezing abruptly.
3. TURN / REACT — small head/body orientation change and reaction acting.

## Locked production rule
- Preserve locked face/design proportions.
- Use clean-keyframe region motion rather than whole-face/body smear warps.
- Build reusable cycles once, then reuse them across scenes and future episodes.
- Favor limited-animation readability over expensive smoothness.
- Add motion where it improves story/comedy/comprehension; do not animate everything constantly.

## Result
PASS. Johnny and Tooth can now move through a repeatable walk-stop-turn pattern that can be integrated into the hallway staging and reused later.
