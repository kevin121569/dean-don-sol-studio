## Production rule reinforced

Movement must belong to thought, dialogue, or reaction. Do not animate merely to keep a character in constant motion.

Preferred acting sequence:

**eye lead -> head/body preparation -> gesture/action -> settle**

Small motivated motion is better than continuous puppet wiggle.
