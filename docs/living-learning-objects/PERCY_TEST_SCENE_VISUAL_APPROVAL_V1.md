# PERCY TEST SCENE — VISUAL APPROVAL v1

Status: **APPROVED PRODUCTION TARGET**
Tracked by Issue #23 / PR #24.

## Approval

The current Percy/Clipper 30–45 second test-scene production board is approved as the visual target for the first real animation proof.

## Locked direction

- Percy appears as a normal, friendly illustrated girl in everyday mode.
- Her head/face transforms into the paired-parentheses working form when her function is needed.
- The parentheses transformation remains simple, readable, and cheap to animate.
- Clipper is approved in his current Grandpa paper-clip design.
- Style remains hand-drawn / illustrated, clean, warm, expressive, and selectively colored.
- Bodies are simple support structures; character identity and personality are primary.
- Clothing/accessories are allowed when they help personality and do not create unnecessary animation burden.
- Motion stays limited: held poses, eye/blink/mouth changes, small gestures, short translations, and simple camera moves.
- Voice, writing, timing, music, and sound carry most of the emotional weight.
- The approved test scene is the template for proving the production pipeline before expanding to more episodes.

## Test-scene beat lock

The first production proof should remain approximately 30–45 seconds and include:

1. Normal Percy and Clipper at the Sorting Facility.
2. Four Treasure Trail cards begin separating.
3. Percy cannot hold them together in normal form.
4. Percy opens/transforms into her parentheses working form.
5. Percy contains the related cards and says: **“I’ve got you.”**
6. Clipper reacts with quiet pride / warmth.

## Production objective

The goal is not visual spectacle. The goal is to prove that the series can be produced repeatedly with simple art, limited motion, strong voices, and strong writing.

If a later design or animation treatment becomes more elaborate than this approved target without improving story clarity, character, or emotion, simplify back toward this reference.
