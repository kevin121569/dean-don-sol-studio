## COLD OPEN

### SCENE 1 — THE BUSIEST TABLE IN THE ROOM

**INT. LEARNING LAB — WORKTABLE — DAY**

A bright worktable is covered with four oversized TREASURE TRAIL cards, scraps of paper, a ruler, notes, clips and markers.

A little sign reads:

**TREASURE TRAIL — FOR TODAY’S VISITOR**

PENCIL leans over CARD ONE, drawing the title in quick confident strokes.

**PENCIL**  
Treasure Trail. Big T. Big T. Tiny dramatic swoosh.

ERASER leans in.

**ERASER**  
Your swoosh ate “Trail.”

Pencil looks.

It did.

**PENCIL**  
It was hungry.

Eraser rubs away the swoosh.

**PENCIL**  
Hey.

**ERASER**  
You can make another one.

Pencil thinks about that.

**PENCIL**  
Smaller swoosh.

He redraws the word, cleaner this time.

Nearby, RULER lines up the edges of Cards Two and Three.

**RULER**  
Two millimeters off.

CURSOR hovers over two layout cards labeled A and B.

**CURSOR**  
Left layout or right layout?

**HUE**  
Left!

Hue slashes yellow across nearly the entire left option.

Everyone stares.

Hue slowly stops.

**HUE**  
Or... less left.

He highlights only the little arrow that matters.

STICKY NOTE sticks herself to the table edge.

Written on her face:

**CARD 3 POINTS LEFT**

**STICKY NOTE**  
I’m keeping this. Nobody lose me.

CLIPPER, an old paper clip with a permanent little bend in his back, struggles to fasten a reference sheet to the packet.

**CLIPPER**  
Hold still, you slippery little—

SNAP.

He gets it.

**CLIPPER**  
There.

MOBI rests nearby, unmistakably a mobile phone. His screen is quiet.

PERCY stands around the title card: one graceful parenthesis on each side.

The arrangement is genuinely lovely.

Pencil leans back.

**PENCIL**  
Okay. That looks beautiful.

Percy brightens.

**PERCY**  
Finally, someone with eyes.

Everyone immediately goes back to work.

Percy keeps smiling.

Then she notices.

Pencil is drawing. Eraser is fixing. Ruler is measuring. Hue is marking. Clipper is clipping. Sticky is remembering. Cursor is choosing.

Percy is still standing there looking beautiful.

Her smile doesn’t disappear.

It just gets a little quieter.

**TITLE CARD: LIVING LEARNING OBJECTS — “I’VE GOT YOU”**

### SCENE 2 — GRANDPA’S BENT LITTLE JOB

Clipper has found a second stack of pages.

Too thick.

He tries one side.

Nope.

The other side.

Nope.

He bends himself farther.

**PERCY**  
Grandpa, bend any more and you’re going to be a staple.

**CLIPPER**  
Staples punch holes in people.

**PERCY**  
Very aggressive family.

Clipper grunts and tries again.

**CLIPPER**  
Ancient technology. Still working.

**PERCY**  
Define “working.”

Clipper finally gets the stack clipped.

He lets go.

The pages stay together.

He gives Percy a look.

**CLIPPER**  
Working.

Percy glances down at herself.

**PERCY**  
What do parentheses do when the pages want to wander?

Clipper answers without drama.

**CLIPPER**  
Group things.

Percy looks at the neat title sitting between her two halves.

**PERCY**  
So... decoration with grammar.

Clipper SNORTS.

**CLIPPER**  
You said it. Not me.

Percy gives him a royal glare.

He returns to his stack.

The joke lands.

The question stays.

## ACT ONE

### SCENE 3 — THE PUFF

A STATUS LIGHT on the room’s PAPER TRANSFER SYSTEM changes from green to amber.

A mechanical CHIME.

**RULER**  
Transfer cycle.

Pencil looks at the four loose cards.

**PENCIL**  
Oh.

A pressure flap opens.

**WHUFF!**

All four cards leap off the table.

**EVERYONE**  
Whoa!

CARD ONE smacks flat against Hue’s face.

**HUE**  
I found one!

Card One peels off and drops behind him.

CARD TWO sails under Ruler.

CARD THREE and CARD FOUR land on the moving transfer surface.

Diverter gates begin shifting.

**CLIPPER**  
Move, move, move!

The characters race along the safe outer edge of the system.

Percy instinctively opens her two halves wide.

But the cards are already too far apart.

Nothing happens.

She reaches a little farther.

Still nothing.

Card Three disappears left.

Card Four shoots past a blue diverter.

**PERCY**  
No, no—

The transfer system continues its automatic cycle.

The group stops at the safety boundary.

Nobody reaches into the moving machinery.

The cards vanish from sight.

A beat.

**PENCIL**  
Please tell me somebody saw where they went.

Everyone starts talking at once.

---

### SCENE 4 — WHAT DO WE ACTUALLY KNOW?

**HUE**  
One went left.

**RULER**  
One went under me.

**CURSOR**  
I think Four went blue.

**STICKY NOTE**  
Three points left.

**PENCIL**  
One bounced off Hue.

**HUE**  
Assaulted me.

Everyone turns toward Mobi.

Mobi’s screen wakes.

**MOBI**  
What are you asking?

**PENCIL**  
Where are the cards?

A beat.

**MOBI**  
I don’t know.

Everyone stares.

**HUE**  
You’re a phone.

**MOBI**  
Yes.

**HUE**  
A smart phone.

**MOBI**  
Still yes.

**HUE**  
So be... more smart.

Mobi’s screen shows a simple list.

**MOBI**  
I can tell you what we actually saw.

He reads.

**MOBI (CONT'D)**  
One hit Hue. Two passed under Ruler. Three disappeared before the split. Nobody saw where Four ended up.

**CURSOR**  
I thought Four went blue.

**MOBI**  
Maybe. But we didn’t see where it finished.

Cursor nods.

**MOBI (CONT'D)**  
What can we check?

That is enough.

The group stops arguing and starts looking.

---

### SCENE 5 — EVERYBODY HAS A MOVE

FAST, PLAYFUL SEARCH SEQUENCE.

Hue scans the transfer markings.

Gray, gray, gray—

A tiny printed ARROW catches light.

Hue marks only the arrow.

**HUE**  
There.

Ruler measures the width of two safe return channels.

**RULER**  
Card cannot pass Channel B while flat.

**PENCIL**  
So not B.

**ERASER**  
Goodbye, B.

Eraser rubs B off Pencil’s rough route map.

Cursor hovers over the two remaining paths.

**CURSOR**  
Left return first?

Sticky Note hops up.

**STICKY NOTE**  
Card Three points LEFT.

**CURSOR**  
Left return first.

CLICK.

The group dashes left.

Percy follows.

She looks for something to do.

Everyone else seems to find one before she does.

---

### SCENE 6 — THE PRETTY JOB

At an accessible staging tray, the group recovers CARD ONE and CARD THREE.

They place them side by side.

**PENCIL**  
Two down.

Percy steps around the two cards.

For a moment, it looks right.

Then a GUIDE ARM begins moving toward the tray.

**RULER**  
Percy.

**PERCY**  
I know. I’m grouping them.

**RULER**  
You’re also where the guide arm goes.

Percy looks.

He is right.

**PERCY**  
Oh.

She steps aside.

The guide arm passes safely.

The cards continue through.

**HUE**  
Progress!

Percy watches the two cards move on without her.

She notices a tiny pair of printed parentheses on the corner of Card Three.

She unconsciously straightens her own two halves.

Then follows.

## ACT TWO

### SCENE 7 — CLIPPER CAN HOLD TWO

On an angled staging board, Cards One and Three begin sliding apart.

Clipper dives in.

SNAP.

He fastens them together.

They stop moving.

**PERCY**  
Ancient technology.

**CLIPPER**  
Working.

Percy smiles despite herself.

A moment later, the group recovers CARD TWO and brings it onto the same board.

Clipper tries to add it.

He clips one corner.

The front text is covered.

**HUE**  
You covered the direction.

Clipper shifts.

Now another card is upside down.

He tries a different angle.

Worse.

Finally he releases the arrangement.

**CLIPPER**  
I can fasten pages.

He points to the three cards.

**CLIPPER (CONT'D)**  
I can’t make every page into one page.

Percy studies him.

He is still useful.

He just has a limit.

---

### SCENE 8 — FALSE FINISH

A return pocket clicks open during a normal safe pause.

CARD FOUR slides into view.

**STICKY NOTE**  
Four!

**HUE**  
Four!

**PENCIL**  
FOUR!

**RULER**  
We all see Four.

They laugh and hurry it onto the final transfer tray.

All four cards are finally arranged in sequence.

Card One: START AT THE RED SHELF.

Card Two: FIND THE QUESTION.

Card Three: TURN LEFT AT THE CLOCK.

Card Four: THE ANSWER IS WHERE THE STORY BEGAN.

Pencil exhales.

**PENCIL**  
We have it.

Everyone cheers.

Then—

**CLICK.**

The transfer system’s warning tone.

A visible panel beside the tray lights up with two OUTBOUND BIN arrows.

The tray begins to tilt toward the final junction.

The four loose cards start sliding apart.

**PENCIL**  
We do not have it.

Clipper lunges and catches two.

**CLIPPER**  
I’ve got these!

The other two drift away.

Hue highlights Card One.

**HUE**  
This one starts it!

**ERASER**  
That does not stop it moving!

Ruler watches the gap widen.

**RULER**  
They are separating at—

Everyone looks at him.

**RULER**  
...a very measurable speed.

Beat.

**RULER (CONT'D)**  
Not useful right now.

Pencil points to the lit arrows on the panel.

**PENCIL**  
If they hit that split separately, they go to different bins.

**CURSOR**  
Can we stop the cycle?

Ruler checks the status display.

**RULER**  
Not from here.

The tray reaches its final indexed pause.

A few seconds before the last pulse.

The group crowds around the accessible tray.

---

### SCENE 9 — EVERYBODY DOES SOMETHING

Clipper tries another configuration.

Two cards stay together.

Two don’t.

He shifts.

Now he blocks a route mark.

Percy watches.

Hue braces one edge with his marker body.

Pencil holds another.

Ruler keeps the row aligned.

Everybody has a job.

Percy stands just outside the cluster.

Quietly:

**PERCY**  
Everybody does something.

Clipper does not look up.

**CLIPPER**  
So do you.

Percy waits.

Nothing.

**PERCY**  
That sounded like the beginning of a wise Grandpa speech.

**CLIPPER**  
Wasn’t.

He wrestles another card into place.

**PERCY**  
You don’t know the rest?

Clipper finally looks at her.

**CLIPPER**  
You think I came out of the box knowing everything about being bent?

Percy almost laughs.

A WARNING CLICK.

Everyone snaps back to the tray.

---

### SCENE 10 — THE RELATIONSHIP BECOMES THE PROBLEM

The four cards are together.

For the first time, the whole sequence is visible at once.

Card One leads to Two.

Two leads to Three.

Three leads to Four.

Four points back to the beginning.

**PENCIL**  
Card One starts it!

**STICKY NOTE**  
Three points left!

**HUE**  
The question and answer stay together!

**CURSOR**  
We need the whole trail!

Percy looks across the cards.

Not four loose pieces.

One set.

Her gaze shifts to Clipper.

He is holding two pages together with his entire little metal body.

Then Percy looks at her own two halves.

A left parenthesis.

A right parenthesis.

Recognition.

Nobody tells her what to do.

---

### SCENE 11 — PERCY MOVES

The machine gives its final PRE-CYCLE CHIME.

Percy steps forward.

Her two parenthesis halves separate.

Everyone notices.

No glow.

No sparks.

No forcefield.

Just Percy moving.

One half takes position to the left of the four cards.

The other moves to the right.

The full set is already on the tray.

Percy closes inward.

Not crushing them.

Not covering the words.

Just enough.

The machine resumes.

The tray tilts.

The cards slide.

Their outside edges meet Percy’s curved halves.

Instead of scattering, they stay together.

The image is unmistakable:

`( 1   2   3   4 )`

Everyone goes silent.

Percy does too.

She looks almost startled by herself.

The tray continues through the transfer path.

---

### SCENE 12 — I’VE GOT YOU

Card Four starts slipping under the curve of Percy’s right half.

**PENCIL**  
Four!

Percy adjusts.

She moves the right parenthesis inward.

The card catches safely against her curve.

She looks down at it.

Then at all four cards held between her two halves.

A small breath.

**PERCY**  
I’ve got you.

Soft.

Certain.

The grouped set crosses the junction intact.

The diverters move beneath them.

Nothing separates.

The packet reaches the receiving tray as one complete set.

A beat of silence.

Then—

**HUE**  
PERCY!

The room erupts.

Pencil jumps.

Sticky Note flaps wildly.

Cursor clicks back and forth like applause.

Ruler actually smiles.

Clipper just looks at Percy.

Proud.

He says nothing.

Not yet.

## ACT THREE

### SCENE 13 — NOW EVERYBODY CAN FINISH

At the receiving tray, Percy keeps the cards gently bracketed.

**PERCY**  
Okay.

She looks at the others.

**PERCY (CONT'D)**  
Now what?

The team dives back in.

**HUE**  
Now we find what matters.

Hue marks the exact phrase on Card Four:

**WHERE THE STORY BEGAN.**

**RULER**  
Red Shelf is six units from the clock. Blue Shelf is nine.

**PENCIL**  
So red.

Pencil completes a missing directional mark.

Eraser rubs away the group’s earlier note pointing to blue.

**ERASER**  
Wrong one.

**PENCIL**  
Better one.

Sticky Note hops forward.

**STICKY NOTE**  
Card Three points LEFT.

Cursor hovers over two route arrows.

**CURSOR**  
Left route?

Everyone checks the cards.

CLICK.

**CURSOR**  
Left route.

Mobi’s screen updates with the route they just established.

**MOBI**  
That fits everything we found.

He looks, in his phone-like way, at the full packet.

**MOBI (CONT'D)**  
Whole trail.

Percy looks at the cards between her two halves.

Then at the group around her.

**PERCY**  
Whole trail.

---

### SCENE 14 — GRANDPA KNOWS THAT LOOK

The finished packet returns to the main worktable.

Percy slowly opens her parentheses.

The cards stay neatly together.

She looks down at herself.

Left curve.

Right curve.

Same Percy.

Not the same understanding.

Clipper approaches.

He straightens as much as a bent paper clip can straighten.

Long side-eye.

**CLIPPER**  
Huh.

Percy looks at him.

**CLIPPER (CONT'D)**  
Knew you were good for more than decoration.

Percy gasps in mock offense.

**PERCY**  
Grandpa.

**CLIPPER**  
What?

**PERCY**  
I am still excellent decoration.

Clipper nods solemnly.

**CLIPPER**  
Very shiny.

Percy gives him a royal glare.

Then one parenthesis leans lightly against him.

Clipper pretends not to notice.

His smile gives him away.

---

### SCENE 15 — THE SPACE AROUND THE THING

The Treasure Trail packet is complete.

Pencil places the title card on top.

Eraser brushes away one last crumb.

Sticky Note settles near the edge.

Ruler checks the stack.

Cursor rests beside the route marker.

Clipper fastens the reference sheet.

Mobi goes quiet again.

No speech.

No ceremony.

The group naturally arranges itself around the finished work.

Percy takes her place around the packet.

This time it does not look like decoration.

It looks like belonging.

Hue steps forward, emotional.

**HUE**  
This is important.

He highlights the title.

Then the border.

Then the subtitle.

Then half the first card.

Everyone slowly turns toward him.

Hue freezes.

Looks at the enormous yellow mess.

Looks at everyone.

**HUE**  
Too much important?

Beat.

Percy raises one parenthesis.

**PERCY**  
A little.

CUT TO BLACK.

**END OF EPISODE 001**

## RED-TEAM REVISION NOTES

Changes from v1:

- removed the implication that Mobi has privileged access to transfer-system control or routing data,
- Mobi now speaks only from observations available to the group,
- moved the final-junction warning to a visible room panel and the other characters’ direct reading of it,
- trimmed explicit “observation vs. guess” teaching language so the distinction is modeled rather than lectured,
- shortened several lines to sound more natural for the 7–11 target audience,
- preserved Percy’s failed early reach so the later physical-span rule is visually established before the discovery,
- preserved Clipper’s usefulness without turning his limit into incompetence,
- kept “I’ve got you” quiet and action-earned before celebration,
- retained the Hue button at the end as tension release after Percy’s emotional beat has fully landed.

## LOCK CHECK

PASS conditions retained:

1. Percy only brackets a relationship the story has already established.
2. The full set is physically together before Percy acts.
3. Percy never remote-grabs or tethers a card.
4. The visible parenthesis shapes are mechanically necessary to the action.
5. Mobi never reveals unseen information or controls the room system.
6. The group solves the episode together after Percy’s discovery.
7. No educational definition interrupts the adventure.
8. “I’ve got you” occurs after containment starts and before celebration.
9. Clipper’s final line echoes Percy’s own “decoration” joke and is played as affectionate family teasing.
10. No live child-facing AI product behavior is authorized by the script.
