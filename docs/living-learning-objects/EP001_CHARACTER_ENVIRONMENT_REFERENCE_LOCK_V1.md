## MASTER ART RULE

**Material coherence, not mascot coherence.**

Every character must still read first as the object or symbol they are.

Do not force a shared face template, identical limbs, identical eyes, identical mouths, gloves, shoes, or generic mascot proportions onto the cast. Personality may enrich the object; it may not disguise it.

The production question is always:

> If the face/acting layer disappeared for one frame, would the audience still know what object this is?

If not, the design has drifted.

# CHARACTER REFERENCE LOCKS

## PERCY — PARENTHESIS PRINCESS

### Canonical form
- One character represented by **two coordinated parenthesis halves**: `( )`.
- The two halves are not twins and are never treated as separate characters.
- Canonical base silhouette comes from the production Percy SVG paths:
  - left curve: `M14 4 C 6 18, 6 42, 14 56 C 11 40, 11 20, 14 4 Z`
  - right curve: `M6 4 C 14 18, 14 42, 6 56 C 9 40, 9 20, 6 4 Z`
- Canonical settled color: **navy `#1A2B4C`**.
- No crown is required to communicate “Princess.” Royal attitude comes through posture, timing, and expression, not costume clutter.

### Fixed-size rule
- Both parenthesis halves retain the same established height/width through Episode 001.
- They may translate, separate, close, and rotate slightly.
- They may not grow, stretch, scale, morph, become brackets/braces, or produce invisible reach.

### Acting
- Percy acts as one mind through two coordinated shapes.
- Her eyeline/face treatment, if used, must remain visually subordinate to the parenthesis silhouette.
- Elegant posture is allowed; generic humanoid body conversion is not.
- Beauty remains part of her self-image. The episode does not require a visual “make-under.”

### Discovery lock
The hero image must read literally as:

`( 1  2  3  4 )`

The cards fit between her normal-size halves. The halves make visible physical contact with outside card edges as the carrier tilts.

## CLIPPER — PAPER CLIP / PERCY'S GRANDPA

### Canonical form
- Production v1 paper clip silhouette: narrow silver-gray clip, **`#B8C0C8`** family.
- Outer form approximately 16×40 relative design units with inner return loop.
- Permanent slight bend communicates age/personality but must not stop him reading instantly as a paper clip.
- No transformation into a hook, staple, chain, carabiner, or generic metal character.

### Acting
- Practical, economical movement.
- Physical comedy comes from flex, angle, and stubborn effort.
- “Old” means experienced and slightly bent, not frail or incompetent.

### Functional proof
When Clipper fastens paper, paper must visibly pass through/under actual clip geometry. The effect cannot be magnetic or magical.

# SCALE RELATIONSHIP LOCK

Exact final pixel scale remains production-dependent, but relative scale is locked by function:

- Percy at normal full height must be tall enough to flank the tightly arranged four-card set without resizing.
- Treasure Trail cards are therefore compact props relative to Percy, not poster-size sheets.
- Clipper must be large enough to visibly fasten two cards/pages but clearly unable to span the full four-card reading arrangement without covering useful information.
- Hue/Ruler/Pencil/Eraser/Sticky/Cursor remain small learning-object peers, not tiny props beside a giant Percy.
- Mobi is phone-sized within the same object world, large enough for other characters to read his screen at conversational distance.

**Production solves fit by card scale and spacing. Percy never changes size to solve fit.**

# TREASURE TRAIL CARD LOCK

Four reusable fixed assets:

1. **CARD 1** — `START AT THE RED SHELF.`
2. **CARD 2** — `FIND THE QUESTION.`
3. **CARD 3** — `TURN LEFT AT THE CLOCK.`
4. **CARD 4** — `THE ANSWER IS WHERE THE STORY BEGAN.`

Rules:
- numbers remain readable and stay attached to the correct text,
- card proportions remain identical,
- text should be composited as stable artwork rather than regenerated frame-by-frame,
- front text stays visible during Percy's containment; her curves contact outside edges rather than covering wording,
- the four cards can be placed closely enough to fit Percy's normal span.

# LEARNING LAB ENVIRONMENT LOCK

## Overall feel
- Warm, bright, curious workroom.
- Real paper/object materials against restrained neutral architecture.
- Magical feeling comes from living objects and clever staging, not fantasy fog or glowing machinery.

## Fixed geography

**LEFT — Worktable / home base**  
Primary assembly surface; warm paper clutter; initial Treasure Trail build; final return image.

**CENTER — Safe transfer interface**  
Accessible exposed trays, return pockets, indexed stops, status panel. Characters can stand and work here during normal pauses.

**RIGHT — Guarded transfer mechanism / outbound split**  
Internal rollers/diverters remain visibly behind guards. Characters do not enter this area.

**BACKGROUND — Route/status information**  
Green/amber cycle light and simple two-bin direction indicators. This is ordinary visible room information, not Mobi data.

## Exposed carrier lock
- Final carrier is a shallow platform moving along the outside face of the guarded system.
- Percy may stand/brace along this accessible carrier path.
- Internal moving hardware remains behind a visible physical barrier.
- No tunnel, chute, enclosed doorway, or machine interior may swallow Percy during the hero sequence.

# ART-DIRECTION LOCK

Preferred visual character:
- tactile 3D/2.5D animated-object world,
- clean readable silhouettes,
- soft material texture,
- restrained cinematic depth of field,
- expressive poses without generic mascot anatomy,
- child-readable staging over visual spectacle.

Avoid:
- glossy toy-commercial plastic across every object,
- giant cartoon eyes pasted identically on every character,
- floating hands/feet that erase object identity,
- superhero VFX,
- random sparkles around Percy,
- cyberpunk Mobi treatment,
- machinery that looks threatening or villainous,
- extreme fisheye/perspective in the Percy reveal.

# PRE-ANIMATION ACCEPTANCE TEST

A locked reference sheet must make all of these statements visually true before motion generation begins:

1. Percy reads immediately as a pair of parentheses and remains one character.
2. Percy's two halves are visibly fixed-size.
3. Clipper reads immediately as a slightly bent paper clip.
4. Every other cast member reads as its native object before personality details.
5. The four cards fit between normal-size Percy halves without stretching her.
6. The Learning Lab clearly separates safe accessible surfaces from guarded moving hardware.
7. Mobi reads as a phone, not an oracle device.
8. The world shares a coherent material/lighting language without forcing identical mascot anatomy.

If any static proof fails one of these, correct the static reference before creating animation.
