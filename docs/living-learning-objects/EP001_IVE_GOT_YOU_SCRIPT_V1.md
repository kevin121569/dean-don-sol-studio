## COLD OPEN

### SCENE 1 — THE BUSIEST TABLE IN THE ROOM

**INT. LEARNING LAB — WORKTABLE — DAY**

A bright worktable is covered with four oversized TREASURE TRAIL cards, scraps of paper, a ruler, notes, clips and markers.

A little sign reads:

**TREASURE TRAIL — FOR TODAY’S VISITOR**

PENCIL leans over CARD ONE, drawing the title in quick confident strokes.

**PENCIL**  
Treasure Trail. Big T. Big T. Tiny dramatic swoosh.

ERASER leans in.

**ERASER**  
Your swoosh ate the word “Trail.”

Pencil looks.

The swoosh has, in fact, eaten half the word.

**PENCIL**  
It was hungry.

Eraser rubs away the swoosh.

**PENCIL**  
Hey.

**ERASER**  
Drafts survive me every day.

Pencil immediately redraws the word, cleaner this time.

Nearby, RULER lines up the edges of Cards Two and Three.

**RULER**  
Two millimeters off.

CURSOR hovers over two layout cards labeled A and B.

**CURSOR**  
Left layout or right layout?

**HUE**  
Left!

Hue slashes yellow across the entire left option.

Everyone stares.

Hue slowly stops.

**HUE**  
Or... a smaller amount of left.

He highlights only the little arrow that matters.

STICKY NOTE sticks herself to the table edge.

Written on her face:

**CARD 3 POINTS LEFT**

**STICKY NOTE**  
I’m keeping this. Nobody lose me.

CLIPPER, an old paper clip with a permanent little bend in his back, struggles to fasten a reference sheet to the packet.

**CLIPPER**  
Hold still, you slippery little—

SNAP.

He gets it.

**CLIPPER**  
There.

MOBI rests nearby, unmistakably a mobile phone. His screen is quiet.

PERCY stands around the title card: one graceful parenthesis on each side.

The arrangement is genuinely lovely.

Pencil leans back.

**PENCIL**  
Okay. That actually looks beautiful.

Percy brightens.

**PERCY**  
Well. Finally, someone with eyes.

Everyone immediately goes back to work.

Percy keeps smiling.

Then she notices.

Pencil creates. Eraser corrects. Ruler measures. Hue focuses. Clipper fastens. Sticky remembers. Cursor chooses.

Percy is still standing there looking beautiful.

Her smile doesn’t disappear.

It just gets a little quieter.

**TITLE CARD: LIVING LEARNING OBJECTS — “I’VE GOT YOU”**

### SCENE 2 — GRANDPA’S BENT LITTLE JOB

Clipper has found a second stack of pages.

Too thick.

He tries one side.

Nope.

The other side.

Nope.

He bends himself farther.

**PERCY**  
Grandpa, if you bend any more, we’re going to have to call you a staple.

**CLIPPER**  
Staples punch holes in people.

**PERCY**  
Very aggressive family.

Clipper grunts and tries again.

**CLIPPER**  
Ancient technology. Still working.

**PERCY**  
Define “working.”

Clipper finally gets the stack clipped.

He lets go.

The pages stay together.

He gives Percy a look.

**CLIPPER**  
Working.

Percy glances down at herself.

**PERCY**  
What do parentheses do when the pages want to wander?

Clipper answers without drama.

**CLIPPER**  
Group things.

Percy looks at the perfectly neat title sitting between her two halves.

**PERCY**  
So... decoration with grammar.

Clipper SNORTS.

**CLIPPER**  
You said it. Not me.

Percy gives him a royal glare.

He returns to his stack.

The joke lands.

The question stays.

## ACT ONE

### SCENE 3 — THE PUFF

A small STATUS LIGHT on the room’s PAPER TRANSFER SYSTEM changes from green to amber.

A mechanical CHIME.

**RULER**  
Transfer cycle.

Pencil looks at the loose Treasure Trail cards.

**PENCIL**  
Oh.

A pressure flap opens.

**WHUFF!**

All four cards leap off the table.

**EVERYONE**  
Whoa!

CARD ONE smacks flat against Hue’s face.

**HUE**  
I found one!

Card One peels off and drops behind him.

CARD TWO sails under Ruler.

CARD THREE and CARD FOUR land on the moving transfer surface.

Diverter gates begin shifting.

**CLIPPER**  
Move, move, move!

The characters race alongside the safe outer edge of the system.

Percy instinctively opens her parentheses wide.

But the cards are already too far apart.

Nothing happens.

She reaches a little farther.

Still nothing.

Card Three disappears left.

Card Four shoots past a blue diverter.

**PERCY**  
No, no—

The transfer system continues through its programmed cycle.

The group stops at the boundary of the moving machinery.

No one reaches in.

No one can simply shut the system down.

The cards are gone from sight.

A beat.

**PENCIL**  
Please tell me someone saw where they went.

Everyone starts talking at once.

---

### SCENE 4 — WHAT DO WE ACTUALLY KNOW?

**HUE**  
One went left.

**RULER**  
One went under me.

**CURSOR**  
I think Four went blue.

**STICKY NOTE**  
Three points left.

**PENCIL**  
I saw one bounce off Hue.

**HUE**  
That one assaulted me.

Everyone turns toward Mobi.

Mobi’s screen wakes.

**MOBI**  
What do you want to know?

**PENCIL**  
Where are the cards?

A beat.

**MOBI**  
I don’t know.

Everyone stares.

**HUE**  
You’re a phone.

**MOBI**  
Correct.

**HUE**  
A smart phone.

**MOBI**  
Also correct.

**HUE**  
So be... more smart.

Mobi’s screen displays four simple lines.

**MOBI**  
Here is what we observed.

He reads them calmly.

**MOBI (CONT'D)**  
Card One hit Hue and fell near the worktable. Card Two passed beneath Ruler. Card Three was last seen before the split. Card Four’s final path was not observed.

**CURSOR**  
I thought Four went past blue.

**MOBI**  
That is a useful possibility. It is not yet a fact.

Cursor nods.

**MOBI (CONT'D)**  
We have observations. We also have guesses.

Beat.

**MOBI (CONT'D)**  
What can we check next?

That changes the room.

The group stops guessing.

They start looking.

### SCENE 5 — EVERYBODY HAS A MOVE

FAST, PLAYFUL SEARCH SEQUENCE.

Hue scans the transfer markings.

Everything is gray, gray, gray—

A tiny printed ARROW catches light.

Hue marks only the arrow.

**HUE**  
There.

Ruler measures the width of two safe return channels.

**RULER**  
Card cannot pass Channel B while flat.

**PENCIL**  
So not B.

**ERASER**  
Goodbye, B.

Eraser rubs B off Pencil’s rough route map.

Cursor hovers over two remaining paths.

**CURSOR**  
Left return first?

Sticky Note hops up.

**STICKY NOTE**  
Card Three points LEFT.

**CURSOR**  
Left return first.

CLICK.

Choice made.

The group dashes left.

Percy follows.

She looks for something to do.

Everyone else seems to find one before she does.

---

### SCENE 6 — THE PRETTY JOB

At an accessible staging tray, the group recovers CARD ONE and CARD THREE.

They place them side by side.

**PENCIL**  
Two down.

Percy steps around the two cards.

For a moment, it looks right.

Then a GUIDE ARM begins moving toward the tray.

**RULER**  
Percy.

**PERCY**  
I know. I’m grouping them.

**RULER**  
You are also standing exactly where the guide arm needs to go.

Percy looks.

He is right.

**PERCY**  
Oh.

She steps aside.

The guide arm passes safely.

The cards continue through.

Everyone cheers.

**HUE**  
Progress!

Percy watches the two cards move on without her.

She notices a tiny pair of printed parentheses on the corner of Card Three.

She unconsciously straightens her own two halves.

Then follows.

## ACT TWO

### SCENE 7 — CLIPPER CAN HOLD TWO

On an angled staging board, Cards One and Three begin sliding apart.

Clipper dives in.

SNAP.

He fastens them together.

They stop moving.

**PERCY**  
Ancient technology.

**CLIPPER**  
Working.

Percy smiles despite herself.

A moment later, the group recovers CARD TWO and brings it onto the same board.

Clipper tries to add it.

He clips one corner.

The front text is covered.

**HUE**  
You covered the direction.

Clipper shifts.

Now another card is upside down.

He tries a different angle.

Worse.

Finally he releases the arrangement.

**CLIPPER**  
I can fasten pages.

He points to the three cards.

**CLIPPER (CONT'D)**  
I can’t make every page into one page.

No shame.

Just a limit.

Percy studies him.

He is still useful.

He is also not the answer to everything.

---

### SCENE 8 — FALSE FINISH

A return pocket clicks open during a normal safe pause.

CARD FOUR slides into view.

**STICKY NOTE**  
Four!

**HUE**  
Four!

**PENCIL**  
FOUR!

**RULER**  
We all see Four.

They laugh and hurry it onto the final transfer tray.

All four cards are finally arranged in correct sequence.

Card One: START AT THE RED SHELF.

Card Two: FIND THE QUESTION.

Card Three: TURN LEFT AT THE CLOCK.

Card Four: THE ANSWER IS WHERE THE STORY BEGAN.

Pencil exhales.

**PENCIL**  
We have it.

Everyone cheers.

Then—

**CLICK.**

The transfer system’s warning tone.

The tray begins to tilt toward the final junction.

The four loose cards start sliding apart.

**PENCIL**  
We do not have it.

Clipper lunges and grabs two.

**CLIPPER**  
I’ve got these!

The other two drift away.

Hue highlights Card One.

**HUE**  
This one starts it!

**ERASER**  
That does not stop it moving!

Ruler watches the gap widen.

**RULER**  
They are separating at—

Everyone looks at him.

**RULER**  
...a very measurable speed.

Beat.

**RULER (CONT'D)**  
Not helpful right now.

Mobi’s screen flashes the upcoming diverter pattern.

**MOBI**  
If the cards cross the final junction separately, they will route to different outbound bins.

**PENCIL**  
Can you stop it?

**MOBI**  
No.

**CURSOR**  
Can you move the cards?

**MOBI**  
Also no.

The tray settles into its final indexed pause.

A few seconds before the last pulse.

The group crowds around the accessible tray.

---

### SCENE 9 — EVERYBODY DOES SOMETHING

Clipper tries another configuration.

Two cards stay together.

Two don’t.

He shifts.

Now he blocks a route mark.

Percy watches.

Hue holds one edge with his marker body.

Pencil braces another.

Ruler tries to keep the row aligned.

Everybody has a job.

Percy is standing just outside the cluster.

Quietly:

**PERCY**  
Everybody does something.

Clipper does not look up.

**CLIPPER**  
So do you.

Percy waits.

Nothing.

**PERCY**  
That sounded like the beginning of a wise Grandpa speech.

**CLIPPER**  
Wasn’t.

He wrestles another card into place.

**PERCY**  
You don’t know the rest?

Clipper finally looks at her.

**CLIPPER**  
You think I came out of the box knowing everything about being bent?

Percy almost laughs.

Almost.

A WARNING CLICK.

Everyone snaps back to the tray.

---

### SCENE 10 — THE RELATIONSHIP BECOMES THE PROBLEM

The four cards are together.

For the first time, their complete relationship is obvious.

Card One leads to Two.

Two leads to Three.

Three leads to Four.

Four points back to the beginning.

**PENCIL**  
Card One starts it!

**STICKY NOTE**  
Three points left!

**HUE**  
The question and answer have to stay together!

**CURSOR**  
We need the whole trail!

Percy’s eyes move across the cards.

Not four pieces.

One set.

Her gaze shifts to Clipper.

He is holding two pages together with his entire little metal body.

Then Percy looks at her own two halves.

A left parenthesis.

A right parenthesis.

Something changes in her expression.

Not confidence.

Recognition.

No one tells her what to do.

---

### SCENE 11 — PERCY MOVES

The machine gives its final PRE-CYCLE CHIME.

Percy steps forward.

Her two parenthesis halves separate.

Everyone notices.

No glow.

No sparks.

No forcefield.

Just Percy moving.

One half takes position to the left of the four cards.

The other moves to the right.

The full set is already on the tray.

Already related.

Already within her span.

Percy closes inward.

Not crushing them.

Not touching the text.

Just enough.

The machine resumes.

The tray tilts.

The cards slide.

Their outside edges meet Percy’s curved halves.

Instead of scattering, they stay together.

The image is unmistakable:

`( 1   2   3   4 )`

Everyone goes silent.

Percy goes silent too.

Her face says the same thing as theirs.

*Did I just do that?*

The tray continues through the transfer path.

---

### SCENE 12 — I’VE GOT YOU

Card Four starts slipping under the curve of Percy’s right half.

**PENCIL**  
Four!

Percy adjusts.

She moves the right parenthesis inward.

The card catches safely against her curve.

She looks down at it.

Then at all four cards held together between her two halves.

A small breath.

No fanfare yet.

**PERCY**  
I’ve got you.

The words are soft.

Certain.

The grouped set crosses the final junction intact.

The diverters move beneath them.

Nothing separates.

The packet reaches the receiving tray as one complete set.

A beat of silence.

Then—

**HUE**  
PERCY!

The room erupts.

Pencil jumps.

Sticky Note flaps wildly.

Cursor clicks back and forth like applause.

Ruler actually smiles.

Clipper just looks at Percy.

Proud.

Very proud.

He says nothing.

Not yet.

## ACT THREE

**PERCY**  
Okay.

**PERCY (CONT'D)**  
Now what?

**HUE**  
Now we find what matters.

**WHERE THE STORY BEGAN.**

**RULER**  
Red Shelf is six units from the clock. Blue Shelf is nine.

**PENCIL**  
So red.

Pencil completes a missing directional mark.

Eraser rubs away the group’s earlier note pointing to blue.

**ERASER**  
We were wrong.

**PENCIL**  
We got better.

Sticky Note hops forward.

**STICKY NOTE**  
Card Three points LEFT.

Cursor hovers over two route arrows.

**CURSOR**  
Left route?

Everyone looks.

The evidence supports it.

CLICK.

**CURSOR**  
Left route.

Mobi’s screen updates.

**MOBI**  
The four cards form one complete route. The evidence supports the left path back to the red shelf.

**MOBI (CONT'D)**  
We have the whole trail.

Percy looks at the cards between her two halves.

Then at the group around her.

**PERCY**  
We do.

### SCENE 14 — GRANDPA KNOWS THAT LOOK

The finished packet is returned to the main worktable.

Percy slowly opens her parentheses.

The cards stay neatly together on the table.

She looks down at herself.

Left curve.

Right curve.

Same Percy.

Not the same understanding.

Clipper approaches.

He straightens as much as a bent paper clip can straighten.

He gives her a long side-eye.

**CLIPPER**  
Huh.

Percy looks at him.

**CLIPPER (CONT'D)**  
Knew you were good for more than decoration.

Percy gasps in mock offense.

**PERCY**  
Grandpa.

**CLIPPER**  
What?

**PERCY**  
I am still excellent decoration.

Clipper nods solemnly.

**CLIPPER**  
Very shiny.

Percy gives him a royal glare.

Then one parenthesis leans lightly against him.

Clipper pretends not to notice.

His smile gives him away.

### SCENE 15 — THE SPACE AROUND THE THING

The Treasure Trail packet is finally complete.

Pencil places the title card on top.

Eraser brushes away one last crumb.

Sticky Note sticks herself near the edge.

Ruler checks the stack.

Cursor rests beside the route marker.

Clipper fastens the reference sheet.

Mobi goes quiet again.

No speech.

No ceremony.

The group naturally arranges itself around the finished work.

Percy takes her place around the packet.

This time it does not look like decoration.

It looks like belonging.

Hue steps forward, emotional.

**HUE**  
This is important.

He highlights the title.

Then the border.

Then the subtitle.

Then half the first card.

Everyone slowly turns toward him.

Hue freezes.

Looks at the enormous yellow mess.

Looks at everyone.

**HUE**  
Too much important?

Beat.

Percy raises one parenthesis.

**PERCY**  
A little.

CUT TO BLACK.

## SCRIPT LOCK NOTES

This draft intentionally preserves the approved development rules:

- Percy’s first extraordinary action is physical bracketing of an already-established related set.
- All four cards are physically together and within Percy’s span before she acts.
- Percy does not attract, tether, teleport, remotely pull, or magically identify any card.
- “I’ve got you” occurs only after her physical containment is already working.
- Clipper remains competent, useful, affectionate, and limited by his actual paper-clip function.
- Mobi explicitly admits uncertainty, distinguishes observations from guesses, asks for the next check, and never solves unseen events.
- The transfer system is room infrastructure outside the learning objects’ control; nobody ignores an obvious stop switch or reaches into moving machinery.
- No character pauses to define the educational concepts for the audience.
- Percy’s beauty is never treated as a flaw she must reject.
- Percy enables the solution but does not replace the team.
- The episode ends on ensemble comedy rather than a moralizing speech.

## NEXT GATE

Before this draft becomes a dialogue/storyboard lock, red-team for:

1. child-natural dialogue,
2. pacing and scene compression,
3. whether any function is demonstrated too mechanically,
4. whether Mobi feels useful but not dominant,
5. whether Percy’s discovery is visually legible without explanation,
6. whether “I’ve got you” lands emotionally without being telegraphed,
7. whether Clipper’s “decoration” line reads affectionate rather than diminishing,
8. whether the final Hue joke releases tension without undercutting Percy’s payoff.
