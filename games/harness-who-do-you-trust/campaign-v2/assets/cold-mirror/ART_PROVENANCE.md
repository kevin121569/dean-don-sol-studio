# The Cold Mirror evidence art

These six images were generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool
for the HARNESS Case 3 development renderer, then resized to 720×432 JPEG for
offline phone use. The Server 4 monitor image was supplied as a **style
reference only**; no Case 1 or Case 2 composition was reused.

Shared direction: polished cinematic semi-realistic 3D illustration; deep navy
and cyan ambient light; restrained amber and red warning accents; one clear clue
per landscape card; family-friendly; no logos, captions, watermarks, fake
branding, or decorative typography.

| Asset | Prompt subject |
|---|---|
| `particle-alert.jpg` | One wall sensor spikes red beside a warm vent and frozen window; no visible smoke. |
| `hvac-trace.jpg` | An electric HVAC system begins a normal high-heat restart with a clear timing trace. |
| `alarm-panel.jpg` | Independent smoke, heat, and carbon-monoxide indicators remain green while a distant air alert glows red. |
| `resident-reports.jpg` | One of three calm shelter residents notices a brief vent odor; nobody is in distress. |
| `service-bulletin.jpg` | An opened sensor, overdue dirty filter, technical interference diagram, serial range, and maintenance calendar. |
| `handheld-check.jpg` | A human fire-safety inspector takes an independent vent reading in the isolated east wing. |

Generated 2026-10-27. The SHA-256 fingerprints used by the renderer and
campaign validator are stored in `campaign.json`.
