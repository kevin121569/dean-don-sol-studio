# The Ledger's Weight art provenance

Generated on 2026-10-27 with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool for the
HARNESS five-case campaign. The existing Cold Mirror sensor image was supplied
only as a palette, lighting, and material reference. Each final asset was
center-cropped and optimized to 720×432 JPEG for the mobile evidence cards.

Shared direction: cinematic 3D editorial evidence art, realistic civic
infrastructure, cool navy/cyan technology light with amber warnings, one clear
focal subject, no logos, readable words, watermarks, fantasy, or split panels.

- `budget-model.jpg`: municipal resource dashboard shifting drainage money to
  a street-cleaning drone program.
- `field-photos.jpg`: rain-dark residential street with clogged storm drains,
  inspection tablet, and evidence flags.
- `rain-forecast.jpg`: regional weather workstation with a wide uncertainty
  cone and one intense-storm spike.
- `cost-sheet.jpg`: city finance desk showing incompatible full allocations and
  a possible smaller compromise.
- `model-notes.jpg`: technical workstation revealing excluded extreme storm
  peaks and a citywide average.
- `drain-inspection.jpg`: pipe camera documenting a severe subsurface blockage.
