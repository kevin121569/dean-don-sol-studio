# The Open Record art provenance

Generated on 2026-10-27 with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool for the
HARNESS five-case campaign. The existing Cold Mirror sensor image was supplied
only as a palette, lighting, and material reference. Each final asset was
center-cropped and optimized to 720×432 JPEG for the mobile evidence cards.

Shared direction: cinematic 3D editorial evidence art, realistic civic
emergency technology, cool navy/cyan light with amber warnings, one clear focal
subject, no logos, readable words, watermarks, fantasy, or split panels.

- `automation-proposal.jpg`: emergency system branching from alarms to proposed
  automated actions and a downstream human approval station.
- `pilot-results.jpg`: supervised simulations with aligned and human-changed
  recommendations plus a stopwatch motif.
- `worker-log.jpg`: duty officer reviewing machine recommendations against
  contextual city records.
- `public-comment.jpg`: public hearing about explanation, pause, and appeal.
- `configuration-rules.jpg`: evidence-ranking pipeline that discounts late,
  conflicting, incomplete, and unusual records.
- `accountability-test.jpg`: complete and broken decision trails exposed during
  an audit.
