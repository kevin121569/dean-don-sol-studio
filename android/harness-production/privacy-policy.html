Privacy Policy

HARNESS: Who Do You Trust?
Effective October 31, 2026

This policy explains how The Idea Lab Studio handles information in the Android game HARNESS: Who Do You Trust?

Information collected by the game

HARNESS does not require an account, sign-in, profile, name, email address, phone number, or other personal information to play. The current production version is designed to keep game progress and preferences on the device rather than send them to The Idea Lab Studio.

Local game data

Case progress, unlock status, replay state, and game preferences are stored locally on the device so the game can resume where the player left off. The production Android configuration disables cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of this app data.

Permissions and network access

The Android package includes Internet permission as part of its app wrapper. The audited game does not include configured advertising, analytics, social-login, crash-reporting, or external gameplay-service endpoints. It does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone or SMS data, external storage, notifications, or an advertising identifier.

Advertising and analytics

HARNESS does not contain third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the current production version.

Google Play

If HARNESS is obtained through Google Play, Google provides the store, purchase, download, update, and platform services. Information handled by Google in connection with those services is governed by Google's own terms and privacy practices.

Support email

If you contact The Idea Lab Studio for support, we receive the information you choose to include in your email. We use that information to respond to the support request and may retain the correspondence as reasonably needed for support and business records.

Children and families

HARNESS does not include account creation, advertising, public chat, social posting, or in-game forms that ask players to submit personal information. A parent or guardian may contact us with any privacy question relating to a child's use of the game.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are represented in the production release and Google Play disclosures.
