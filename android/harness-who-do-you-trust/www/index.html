How to play

You are in charge of Server 4 at Open Forge. An alarm says it may still be sending data after it was cut off from the network. Security wants it shut down, but that would erase clues. Your job is to decide what to do.

  • BOY — the Searcher. Finds information quickly, but doesn't always check it.
  • TOOTH — the Fact-Checker. Checks where reports came from and when, but takes time.
  • LUKE — the Worst-Case Thinker. Spots danger, but may assume the worst.
  • DON SOL — the Team Lead. Connects the clues, but only the ones the others find.

Tap a clue to open it. Ask your helpers what they think, and ask again after you find something new. Choose how much you trust each one: Doubt, Neutral or Trust. Then decide.

There's a deadline in the story, but no real countdown while you play. Take your time. It's okay not to know everything.

Keyboard: Tab moves between controls, Enter or Space activates, Esc closes this dialog.
